How the Print Hub works

Five steps, and only the file moves. Quality is held by certification: approved printers and materials, calibration, inspection, packaging and delivery standards.

01

Join & verify

We verify every lab's equipment, materials, print quality and production standards before it joins.

02

Build your listing

Printers, build sizes, materials, capacity, lead times and the countries you serve: what a seeker's search is matched against.

03

Demand arrives

Search the network yourself, or ask us to recommend the right partner for the job.

04

Printed locally

The receiving lab prints, packages and ships inside the destination market, to the network's standard. Branding and finish are agreed between the two labs.

05

Everyone earns

The lab's own invoice is paid directly, lab to lab; billing never passes through us.

Certification, in plain terms
Approved printers and materials (Nylon 11, Nylon 12, Nylon 12 Tough and Polypropylene) plus calibration, accuracy, inspection, packaging and delivery standards. Hosts warrant a quality service, not just a product.

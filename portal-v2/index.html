Global Mill Hub

Your production line.
Inside someone else's factory.

The world's biggest sportswear brands haven't owned their factories for decades — what they own is a dedicated line, a team and a quality standard inside someone else's. The Mill Hub brings that model to milled orthotics: an established multi-site manufacturer allocates a cell to you alone, trained on your product, your styles, your top covers, your packaging — and held to your standard.

payments Price that changes the sums

Per-pair pricing materially below in-house manufacturing cost in high-wage markets — with design work included, not bolted on. You see the exact number inside a personal introduction.

sync_alt Redundancy, built in

Production mirrored across facilities on different continents. A storm, a materials shortage or a shutdown in one region never reaches your customers — work simply reroutes.

tune Control you keep

One named production manager, as accountable to you as your own factory floor. "Rush this one through", "change how we do the heels" — and it happens. The brand and relationships stay yours.

Built for labs in a time of change.

Any step change — up or down — is the moment this model earns its keep. If one of these is you, the conversation is worth an hour.

How the Print Hub works

Six steps, and only the file moves. Quality is held by certification: approved printers and materials, calibration, inspection, packaging and delivery standards.

01

Join & verify

We verify every lab's equipment, materials, print quality and production standards before it joins.

02

Build your profile

Printers, build sizes, materials, capacity, lead times and the countries you serve: your entry on the capacity map.

03

Demand arrives

Search the network yourself, or ask us to recommend the right partner for the job.

04

The file moves

An encrypted design file transfers securely between members. Nothing physical crosses a border.

05

Printed locally

The receiving lab prints, packages and ships domestically, carrying your branding, to your standard.

06

Everyone earns

The work is paid at the fixed network price. Our small network fee is added on top, never taken from a lab's margin.

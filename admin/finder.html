What the finders look for

Every Saturday and Sunday the finders search the Fit360 database and the web for companies that match the opportunities switched on here, and each find arrives on the Prospects page. Switch one off, or give it an end date, and the finders stop looking for it. Campaigns keep running until you pause them on the Campaigns board.

A deal to find partners for

Name the deal's folder (as under deals/, e.g. bilt-labs). A Claude session drafts its search terms from the deal's profile; you confirm them by switching it on.

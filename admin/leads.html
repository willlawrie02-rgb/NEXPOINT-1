Prospects

Every company the weekend finders surface, the Fit360 campaign list, the existing leads and any company you add arrives here as a prospect. Set its Status, Sector, Company type, Lead quality and Campaign, then Accept: it becomes a lead and goes onto its campaign. A campaign of None makes a lead Chris works personally. Park keeps a company for later; Kill means it is never proposed again. Notes stay with the company for whoever picks it up next. Nothing is created from the browser: the engine acts on your click.

Mill Hub

Hosts offering CNC milling capacity on the left, seekers on the right. Approve or decline each listing from its card, offer a live host to a seeker, and approve, add to or decline each seeker's picks. Nothing is listed, charged or asked of a provider until you do.

Show

Approving a listing makes the site a host on this hub, starts its membership at 1,000 a year or 100 a month (the host's choice; until the form carries it, you are asked when you approve) and puts the anonymised card in front of seekers; the host is emailed once, saying all three. Declining sends the host what to change and shows nothing meanwhile. "Offer to a seeker" on a live host emails that host the offer; approving a seeker's picks asks each of those providers, and nobody else, to accept or decline. No company is ever named to another until the provider accepts the introduction; the seeker accepted its terms when requesting. Introductions live on their own page: Introductions.

Organisations

Every organisation on the Global Hub: the follow-through score and its working, subscription standing, host status and listings per hub, open strikes with their source and cure date, the Pipedrive ids, and fees owed on confirmed but uninvoiced orders. Recording a payment queues the engine to advance the billing period. A company is On, Paused or Removed, and only you or Chris move it: Pause takes it out of search and the desk's pickers with the record kept, Remove takes it off the hub with its people unable to act, and both are reversible here. The engine never changes a state itself; it tells you when a company needs a decision. Curing a strike closes it in the requester's name; an audit strike is the manual way to open one.

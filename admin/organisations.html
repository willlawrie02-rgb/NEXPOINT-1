Accounts

Every account on the Global Portal, for the money: the follow-through score and its working, subscription standing, host status and listings per hub, open strikes with their source and cure date, the Pipedrive ids, and fees owed on confirmed but uninvoiced orders. Each host's membership invoice shows under its subscription: Record payment (by hand) marks it paid when Xero has not matched the payment itself, Extend the clock moves the day an unpaid invoice hides the listing, and Bill from the first introduction, said before approval, starts the billing at the hub's first new introduction. Everything else an account needs is on its company page, opened from its name: Pause and Resume, Remove from the hub and Restore, strikes with Cure and Open audit strike, Delete account and, for a closed or deleted account, Erase the records (Will, 22 October 2026: Contacts replaces this board, which keeps the money until Money, plan 075, takes it). A removed, closed or deleted account drops into the Archive below the register, out of the working list. Closed is the holder's own state: only the holder reopens it, from the account page.

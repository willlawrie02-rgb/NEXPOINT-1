How an introduction actually happens.

The Global Hub shows you what exists. People decide what meets.

01

Pick your hub

Print, mill, opportunities: each hub shows real, current capacity, anonymised but verified by us first-hand.

02

Introduce yourself

Two minutes at the desk: who you are, your volumes, your systems, what you're after. Chris or Will reads every profile personally.

03

A person weighs the fit

One of us reviews every request, usually within two working days. If it isn't right, we'll tell you why and keep looking.

04

A personal introduction

When both sides agree, we introduce you directly: names, faces, facilities. From there the conversation is yours; we stay close to help it land.

visibility_off Discretion as standard

Every hub describes the deal, not the party. Names, prices and references never appear on the Global Hub; they are shared only inside a personal introduction, the way good deals have always been done.

verified Verified, not listed

This is not a marketplace. Every node, every capacity figure and every opportunity is held by NexPoint on behalf of a partner we know personally: capacity we've seen, products we've handled, people we trust.

handshake Introduced, not sold

Nothing is bought here. When something fits your operation, we make a personal introduction under the network's simple terms, so both sides know where they stand, and step back. The relationship, and the deal, is yours.

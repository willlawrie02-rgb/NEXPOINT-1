A cell of your own, without the overhead.

An established multi-site manufacturer allocates a section of its production floor to you alone, trained on your product, your styles, your top covers and your packaging, and held to your standard.

payments Price that changes the sums

Per-pair pricing materially below in-house manufacturing cost in high-wage markets, with design work included in the price. We share the exact per-pair number when we introduce you.

sync_alt Redundancy, built in

Production mirrored across facilities on different continents. If a storm, a materials shortage or a shutdown stops one region, the work moves to another and your customers still get their orders.

tune Control you keep

One named production manager, as accountable to you as your own factory floor. "Rush this one through", "change how we do the heels", and it happens. The brand and the relationships stay yours.

Commercial proposal

What I'd put in the proposal

What David Lloyd gets

  • 12-week trial in 4 London clubs
  • Intro pricing: first case per SKU free, then launch discount
  • Margin at or above their current packaged lager
  • Fridge branding, menus & POS paid for by Prime Time
  • Staff training and member sampling days included

What we agree upfront

  • Supplied through their existing wholesaler, no new admin
  • Eye-level fridge facing + a place on the drinks menu
  • Lager & Zero in all 4 clubs, IPA in 2
  • Shared weekly sales data from the till
  • If KPIs are hit: agreed terms for wider rollout
